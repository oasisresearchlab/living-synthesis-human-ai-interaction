Claude:
> Reading: Almeda et al. (2024), *Prompting for Discovery: Flexible Sense-Making for AI Art-Making with DreamSheets* ([[@almedaPromptingDiscoveryFlexible2024]]). Joel Chan framed the paper as a new kind of problem for the seminar: making sense of an unknown prompt-to-image mapping, not expressing intent to a model you already understand. Discussion covered why a 2D spreadsheet grid (vs. a single prompt box, chat, or a free canvas), how the grid connects to sensemaking theory (learning loop complex, residue, information scent), and the role of training-data knowledge as AI literacy. The main point of contention was the evaluation: the in-the-wild expert study shows artists built rich exploration structures, but not that those structures produced insight, or that the tool pushes people toward systematic thinking rather than suiting experts who already think that way (contrasted with ChainForge).

### Claims
- [[CLM - 2D grids strike a sweet spot between flexibility and structure for comparative sensemaking - 1D lists are too linear to compare and free-form canvases lack systematicity]]
- [[CLM - a single prompt box with regenerate supports neither history nor comparison, chat supports history but only weak comparison, and spreadsheet grids support history, comparison, and reuse]]
- [[CLM - sensemaking tools deliver benefit when the practice they embody matches the existing sensemaking practices of the community using them]]
- [[CLM - steering a generative model first requires making sense of its prompt-to-output mapping, a problem distinct from expressing intent to a model whose behavior is understood]]
- [[CLM - effective prompts for text-to-image diffusion models are keyword lists that are less human-readable than instruction-style LLM prompts]]
- [[CLM - knowledge of a text-to-image model's training data is AI literacy that complements, and cannot easily be gained from, exploring outputs in tools like DreamSheets]]
- [[CLM - what text-to-image models know most about is shaped by what gets captioned online, which is shaped by commercial incentives]]
- [[CLM - for creative uses, a sensemaking tool for generative models succeeds when users find parts of the output space they personally like, not when outputs are objectively better]]
- [[CLM - artifacts that users build during in-the-wild deployments show a tool is used as intended but are weak direct evidence that it produced insight]]
- [[CLM - spreadsheet grids support the sensemaking loop by keeping representations (rows and columns) next to data (cells), so surprising outputs surface as residue]]
- [[CLM - effective text-to-image prompting is craft knowledge of a medium, complementary to systematic claims about what produces better art]]
- [[CLM - public sharing of prompts and outputs in communities of practice, as in Midjourney's Discord, supports collective sensemaking of text-to-image models]]
- [[CLM - constraints in simple creative tools can foster creativity rather than limit it]]
