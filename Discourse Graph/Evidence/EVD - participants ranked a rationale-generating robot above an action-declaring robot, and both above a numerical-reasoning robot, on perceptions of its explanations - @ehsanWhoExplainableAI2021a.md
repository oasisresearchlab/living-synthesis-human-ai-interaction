### How?
> [!info] How were the measures/data collected (i.e., with what **procedures/analyses/experimental design**)? Note: this can be easy to blur with the "what", and that's ok. Focus on getting the details capture, not getting the classification just right.

Claude:
> Participants watched an autonomous robot in a "high stakes space rescue mission" (navigating boulders and lava to reach food) while its explanation played alongside its actions. Three robots: RG (rationale given, natural language with justification, generated by neural machine translation), AD (action declared, natural language without justification), and NR (numerical reasoning, the Q-values of a tabular Q-learning agent). Participants ranked the robots on five measures, then explained their choices; open responses were analyzed with grounded theory (S08, Distiller, SP-24 W07).
