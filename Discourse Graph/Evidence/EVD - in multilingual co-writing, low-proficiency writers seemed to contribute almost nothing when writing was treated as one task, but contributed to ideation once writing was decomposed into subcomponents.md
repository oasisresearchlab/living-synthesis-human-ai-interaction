> From a related study of multilingual co-writing (shared in the seminar), on co-writing among people from different language backgrounds (SP-25 W03 notes): writing tasks are implicitly composed of smaller steps (e.g., ideation of the content, producing words that express the ideas). When participants treated "writing" as a single task, they tended to think that people with low proficiency were making almost no contributions. Once "writing" was dissected into subcomponents, it became clear that regardless of language proficiency, people could contribute to ideation, but they needed a "protected" space to do so without worrying about how to word their ideas.
