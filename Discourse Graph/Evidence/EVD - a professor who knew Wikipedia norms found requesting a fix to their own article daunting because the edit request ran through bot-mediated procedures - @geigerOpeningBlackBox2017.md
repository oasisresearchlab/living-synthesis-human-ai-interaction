> Composite vignette 1 in Geiger's paper, as distilled by S16 (Distiller, SP-24 W08): a professor tried to correct information on their Wikipedia page and found the edit request process daunting and bureaucratic, even though they knew Wikipedia's norms. S16 read it as showing (1) how intertwined systems and governance processes alienate newcomers (newcomer socialization), and (2) opacity arising from complex sociotechnical systems: an edit needs both technical action and navigation of cultural and procedural nuance.
