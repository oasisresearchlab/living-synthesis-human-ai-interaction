## Description
Claude:
> A sociocognitive architecture proposed in [[@guptaFosteringCollectiveIntelligence]] for how collective intelligence (CI) emerges and is maintained, extended to human-AI collectives. It has three transactive systems (S11, Distiller, SP-24 W11):
> - **Transactive memory system**: processes through which collaborators' knowledge and skills, and their beliefs about who knows what, are updated so knowledge goes to and comes from the most appropriate collaborator.
> - **Transactive attention system**: filters, chunks, and processes information, with awareness of other agents' workloads, to reduce coordination costs on interdependent tasks. Collectives must distribute members' "limited and often fragmented attention ... per its importance to the group". Groups with a strong one show "burstiness", a sign of healthy synchronous coordination.
> - **Transactive reasoning system**: aligns cognitive states with the outcomes other agents value in a changing environment.
> These rest on metacognitive processes, including [[PTN - mutual theory of mind between humans and AI agents - machine theory of mind and human theory of machine mind]].
> ![[qp2umUIRt0.png]]
> S11's reading: "Interactions" are external and require the internal "metacognitive processes" that make sense of the environment and other agents.
