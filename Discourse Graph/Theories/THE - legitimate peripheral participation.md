## Description
Claude:
> From communities-of-practice theory (Lave and Wenger): newcomers first participate in low-stakes, peripheral but legitimate ways, learning the community's rules and norms and building reputation, and gradually move toward the center. Learning is **situated** in participation. An experienced member often acts as a **broker** between the community and the newcomer (S05, Connector, SP-24 W08; S05 credits the idea's root to an earlier CSCW seminar).
> A **community of practice** is a group of people in the same activity who learn from each other, share norms, and use similar tools and techniques, e.g., a lab or research center (S13, ELI5 linter, SP-24 W12).
