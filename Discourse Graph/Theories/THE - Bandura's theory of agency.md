(Joel Chan, SP-25 seminar): applied in a related study of machine-translation-mediated conversation (shared in the seminar, during the SP-25 session on [[@zhengEvaluatingNonAIExperts2025]]) on non-native speakers' agency when using machine translation. The study introduced three interaction designs (labeling, regular post-editing, augmented post-editing) in which non-native speakers use varying scopes of language resources to influence machine translation outputs and sustain agency. The "resource" notion was contextualized using prior research on non-native speakers' language resource composition, which led to the three conditions.
