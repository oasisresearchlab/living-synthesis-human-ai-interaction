## Description

Claude:
> A framework from the MIT Data + Feminism Lab (D'Ignazio and Klein) applied to design. Principles as listed in the seminar (S08, Distiller, SP-24 W16): Rethink Binaries; Embrace Pluralism; Examine Power and Aspire to Empowerment; Consider Context; Legitimize Embodiment and Affect; Make Labor Visible.
> - "the most complete knowledge comes from synthesizing multiple perspectives, with priority given to local, Indigenous, and experiential ways of knowing" (Embrace Pluralism)
> - "data — and missing data — are the product of unequal social relations, and this context is critical for accurate and ethical analysis" (Consider Context)
> S13 (SP-24 W16): "All reasonable even to non-feminists. They show up in other epistemologies/disciplines."
