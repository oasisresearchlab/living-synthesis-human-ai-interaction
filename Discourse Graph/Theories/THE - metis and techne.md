## Description

Claude:
> From James C. Scott's *Seeing Like a State*: **techne** is formal, universal, codified knowledge; **metis** is local, practical, tacit knowledge built from experience in a particular setting.
> Joel Chan (Connector, SP-24 W13) used it to read the diabetic retinopathy deployment: the nurses held local metis (previous protocols, the cost of traveling to hospitals, etc.) "that need to be incorporated into the overall system for it to really work". AI systems can act as techne "that cannot 'see' the metis, and kinda squashes it".
