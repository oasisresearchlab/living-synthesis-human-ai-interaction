## Description
Claude:
> Norman's two gulfs between a user and a system. The gulf of execution is the difficulty of turning intentions into actions the system accepts; the gulf of evaluation is the difficulty of telling what state the system is in and whether the goal was met. S01 (🤔 ELI5 linter, SP-25 W11) glossed it as "the difficulty users face when trying to translate their intentions into actions within a system".
