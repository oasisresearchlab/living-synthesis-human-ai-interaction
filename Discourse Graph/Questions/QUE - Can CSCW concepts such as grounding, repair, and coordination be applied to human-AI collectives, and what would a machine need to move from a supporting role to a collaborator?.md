## Context
Claude:
> S05 (Connector, SP-24 W11) asked whether concepts, framings, and methods from CSCW (computers supporting collaboration among human agents) can be applied to study COHUMAIN ([[@guptaFosteringCollectiveIntelligence]]), and what machines need to move from a supporting role to a collaborator. Candidate concepts:
> - Shared mental models and team cognition: transactive memory and attention systems (also in the paper's section 4).
> - Common ground and grounding: Clark and Wilkes-Gibbs 1986, "Referring as a collaborative process"; Homaeian et al. 2021, "Joint Action Storyboards: A Framework for Visualizing Communication Grounding Costs" (https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3449102).
> - Repair by conversational agents: Do et al. 2023, "To Err is AI" (https://doi.org/10.1145/3579532); Ashktorab et al. 2019, "Resilient Chatbots" (https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300484).
> - Situation awareness and coordination, especially remote: Cummings et al. 2009, relational model of coordination delay (https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/isre.1090.0239).
