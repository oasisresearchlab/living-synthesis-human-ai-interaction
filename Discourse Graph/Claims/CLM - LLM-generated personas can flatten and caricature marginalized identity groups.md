## Grounds
Claude:
> - [[@wangLargeLanguageModels2025]]: "Large language models that replace human participants can harmfully misportray and flatten identity groups" (*Nature Machine Intelligence*, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-00986-z). Shared by Joel Chan in SP-25 seminar housekeeping.
> - Related work on steering vs. prompting, [[@veselovskyLocalizedCulturalKnowledge]]: LLMs are poor at local context; prompting with context (e.g., "I am Japanese") helps somewhat but can lead to stereotyping, while adding a linear steering vector helps (40% improvement for cultural localization) without stereotyping. From the author on X: "the generations stay more faithful, less stereotypical, and more diverse than explicit prompting. This leads us to conclude that cultural knowledge is conserved within LLMs without using prompting; we just need new ways of accessing it." (https://x.com/VminVsky/status/1912174727868694783)
