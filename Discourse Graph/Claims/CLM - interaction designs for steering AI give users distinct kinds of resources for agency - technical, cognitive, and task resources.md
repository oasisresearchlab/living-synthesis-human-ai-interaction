## Grounds

Claude:
> S05's tentative categories for what the interaction designs offer users to sustain agency (S05, SP-25 W09 synthesis):
> - **Technical resources**: [[ART - GAM Changer]], via [[PTN - Shared Representation]] and [[PTN - Direct Manipulation]], lets people "monitor and adjust model behavior directly."
> - **Cognitive (thinking) resources**: [[PTN - LLM Chains]] and [[ART - ChainForge]] are "less about given people the technical capabilities to directly manipulate a glassbox model, but to give people the cognitive (or thinking?) resources to examine LLM behavior against multiple changing parameters of LLM prompts and formulate rules or principles for model behavior." [[ART - ConstitutionMaker]] and [[ART - AI Policy Projector]] also fit here.
> - **Task resources**: tools like GAM Changer and LLM chains may also support "how people or groups of people discuss and determine how to use AI tools as well as the task at hand to ensure better outcomes."
> S05 asked whether the distinctions make sense, how to track their effectiveness, and what other kinds of resources exist. (S05, SP-25 W09 synthesis)
