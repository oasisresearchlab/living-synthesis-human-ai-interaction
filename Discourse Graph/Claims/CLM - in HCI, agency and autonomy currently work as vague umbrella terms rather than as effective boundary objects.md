## Grounds
Claude:
> - Bennett et al. find that agency and autonomy figure "not as effective boundary objects in the HCI landscape, but as vague umbrella constructs ... subsuming a surprising diversity of understandings and theoretical underpinnings" (p. 2, from S13's annotations in [[@bennett2023howa]]; session reading [[@bennettHowDoesHCI2023]]).
> - S05 (Distiller, SP-24 W12) put the tension as: flexibility of meaning serves each community's needs, but a lack of consistent meaning blocks robust understanding. The review argues HCI should move agency and autonomy from umbrella concepts to boundary objects.
> ![[uSgTkVBVqK.png]]
> - [[EVD - only about a third of 161 CHI papers on autonomy or agency gave an explicit definition of either term - @bennettHowDoesHCI2023]].
> - Lineage (S08 and S11, Archaeologists, SP-24 W12): the authors use Star's "This is not a boundary object" (2010), on boundary objects being both local and integrative. S11 added Akkerman and Bakker's "Boundary Crossing and Boundary Objects" (2011), which in S11's view makes the benefits of the shift more explicit.
