## Grounds
Claude:
> - Joel Chan's consolidated notes on [[@jiangGraphologueExploringLarge2023]] (Distiller, SP-24 W03): "linear chat format is suboptimal interaction medium for people to use to **control** intelligent assistance". The paper's hypothesis is that a dynamically created graph of responses is both more effective to learn from and "a more __controllable__ interaction medium".
> - Joel Chan singled out the paper's section 9.4.3 as the most interesting result, validating the hypothesis that the graph is a more natural medium for *controllable* intelligent assistance. See [[EVD - study participants experienced linear ChatGPT chat as hard to control for information-seeking operations - @jiangGraphologueExploringLarge2023]].
> - The [[PTN - Direct Manipulation]] node records this as the concept that explains why linear chat is a poor medium for control, though the paper did not cite it.
> - S11 (W01) made a broader point: the generic chat interface "is supposed to work for all use cases, but this is definitely not the only thing we can use AI for".
