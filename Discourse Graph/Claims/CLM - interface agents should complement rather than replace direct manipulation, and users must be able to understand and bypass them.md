## Grounds
Claude:
> Pattie Maes in her 1990s debate with Ben Shneiderman ([[@shneidermanDirectManipulationVs1997]]), quoted in Joel Chan's announcements (SP-25 W04 notes): "there are real limits to what we can do with visualization and direct manipulation because our computer environments are becoming more and more complex. We cannot just add more and more sliders and buttons ... we will have to, to some extent, delegate certain tasks or certain parts of tasks to agents ... However, this is completely a complementary technique to well-designed interfaces — visualization, and direct manipulation — not a replacement. Users still need to be able to bypass the agent if they want to do that ... The user has to be able to understand what the agent does."
> - Joel flagged the debate as "very, very relevant to today ... where people think a lot about like agent, you know, AI" (SP-25 W04, [00:01:52]–[00:02:18]), and as relevant to direct-manipulation trade-offs for novices (notes).
