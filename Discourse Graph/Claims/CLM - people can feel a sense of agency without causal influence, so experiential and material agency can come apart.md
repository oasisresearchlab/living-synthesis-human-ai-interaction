## Grounds
Claude:
> - Bennett et al.: sense of agency, the "implicit sense of authorship of an action" (operationalized via intentional binding), can be manipulated through sense of ownership, haptic feedback, or timing, and users can feel agency where they have no causal influence (pp. 7-8, per [[@bennett2023howa]]).
> - S05 (Distiller, SP-24 W12) separated "material" from "experiential" agency and asked: "If you are to study agency and autonomy, would you prioritize the material or experiential aspect or both?"
> - An embodied, experiential line of work was on S05's proposed reading list: "I did that! Measuring Users' Experience of Agency in their own Actions" (CHI 2012, https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2207676.2208350), focused on how physical interaction modalities shape the experience of agency (S05, SP-24 W10).
## Other Notes
Claude:
> - Design implication worth testing: interfaces could make users feel in control while giving them little real influence, so measures of felt agency are not enough to show that a design sustains agency.
