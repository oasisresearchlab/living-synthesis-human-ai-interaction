> Raised in the SP-25 seminar discussion of what we know about how well [[ART - DreamSheets]] works for people:
> - Knowing how text-to-image models work "gives a richer source of hypotheses to test in dream sheets," because those hypotheses can be added as rows or columns to see whether they change the output. (SP-25 seminar discussion)
> - Example: the timeframe of the training data. If image collection stopped around 2020–2021, the language and metadata in the training data would differ from what people use now, since internet language keeps changing (e.g., Gen Z slang, "brat summer"). Knowing that would help users choose what to try in their prompts. (SP-25 seminar discussion)
