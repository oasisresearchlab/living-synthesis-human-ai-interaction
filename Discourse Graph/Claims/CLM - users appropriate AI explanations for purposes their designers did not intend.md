## Grounds
Claude:
> [[EVD - participants read AI explanations as serving functions beyond their design, such as confirming normal operation or enabling debugging - @ehsanWhoExplainableAI2021a]]: in [[@ehsanWhoExplainableAI2021a]], non-AI participants took action declarations as confirmation that things were working, and AI participants read numbers as debugging aids. The paper calls this appropriation: "usage patterns that go beyond the original designers' expectations" (p. 5, quoted by S11, SP-24 W07), drawing on [[@dixDesigningAppropriation2007]].
> The paper frames explanations as both "product and process": the explanation itself, and the process of making sense of it (S08, Distiller, SP-24 W07).
