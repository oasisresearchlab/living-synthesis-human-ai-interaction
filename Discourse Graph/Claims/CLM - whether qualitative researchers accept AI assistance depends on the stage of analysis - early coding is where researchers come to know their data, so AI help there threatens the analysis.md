## Grounds

> - Feuston and Brubaker's abstract: "the stage of qualitative analysis matters for how scholars believe AI can and should be used"; scholars "are amenable to working with AI in diverse ways, such as for data exploration and coding, as long as it assists rather than automates their analytic work practice" ([[@feustonPuttingToolsTheir2021]]).
> - [[EVD - many qualitative scholars interviewed resisted AI suggestions at the start of analysis because those steps are how they come to know their data - @feustonPuttingToolsTheir2021]] (S11, Academic Researcher, SP-24 W10).
> - S05 (Connector, SP-24 W10) proposed a similar split: exploratory analysis may suit AI more, while focused analysis needs human insight. They also asked about open vs. axial coding.
> - [[EVD - qualitative scholars saw value in AI that surfaces low-frequency items and the tails and shape of a large corpus to guide sampling and exploration - @feustonPuttingToolsTheir2021]] shows acceptance at the sampling stage.
