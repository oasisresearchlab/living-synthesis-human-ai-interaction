> - The passage S11 quoted defines appropriation: people "do not 'play to the rules': they adapt and adopt the technology around them in ways the designers never envisaged" (e.g., a screwdriver to open a paint tin, emailing yourself links instead of bookmarking). "These improvisations and adaptations around technology are not a sign of failure, things the designer forgot, but show that the technology has been domesticated ... At this point we know the technology has become the users' own not simply what the designer gave to them. This is appropriation."
