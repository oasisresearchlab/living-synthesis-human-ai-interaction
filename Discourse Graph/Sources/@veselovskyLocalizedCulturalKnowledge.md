## Discussion notes
Claude:
> - Shared under "cool stuff" at the start of the SP-25 Week 12 session (on [[@vaithilingamDynaVisDynamicallySynthesized2024]]) as a new paper on steering vs. prompting: LLMs do poorly with localized cultural knowledge; prompting with context (e.g., "I am Japanese") helps somewhat but can lead to stereotyping; adding a linear steering vector helps (a reported 40% improvement for cultural localization) without the stereotyping.
> - The author's own summary (Veniamin Veselovsky, on X): steered generations "stay more faithful, less stereotypical, and more diverse than explicit prompting," which suggests that "cultural knowledge is conserved within LLMs without using prompting; we just need new ways of accessing it." https://x.com/VminVsky/status/1912174727868694783
