## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Explanations work relative to what people already believe. S08 (SP-24 W06) proposed:
> 1. Elicit initial **mental models**: ask students and teachers to explain their current understanding of the model, "to get a sense of the mental models you will be 'interpreting' to."
> 2. Have developers explain the model to teachers, then ask "**what questions remain**, where are their explanatory needs?"
> S08 also suggested surveying extraneous variables (other help students got; whether the system encourages collaboration) and pointed to the sociotechnical gap in XAI: Ehsan, Saha, De Choudhury, and Riedl (2023), "Charting the Sociotechnical Gap in Explainable AI" (CSCW).
