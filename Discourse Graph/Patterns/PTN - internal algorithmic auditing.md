## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Audits run inside the organization building the AI system, across its development lifecycle, to complement external audits. Internal auditors get direct access to intermediate processes of building the system. Audits are "tools for interrogating complex processes, often to determine whether they comply with company policy, industry standards or regulations". Because results depend on human judgment, audit integrity requires a rigorous procedure ("procedural justice"). (S05 Distiller and S08 ELI5 linter, SP-24 W14, from [[@rajiClosingAIAccountability2020]])
> - Accountability is "the state of being responsible or answerable for a system, its behavior and its potential impacts"; governance holds systems accountable by proxy because they are not "moral or legal agents" themselves. (S05, S08; Joel Chan highlighting "agents", SP-24 W14)
