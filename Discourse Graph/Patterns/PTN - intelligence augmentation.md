## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.
Claude:
> Intelligence augmentation (IA): "using computers to extend people's ability to process information and reason about complex problems". [[@heerAgencyAutomationDesigning2019]] (p. 1) contrasts it with AI ("developing computational methods for perception, reasoning, and action"). The two programs share midcentury origins but have "charted largely separate trajectories". IA, "beginning with the work of early luminaries such as Douglas Engelbart and Alan Kay", led to the personal computer, GUIs, the web browser, and touch devices. (Quoted by Joel Chan, 🕸️ Connector, SP-24 W04; definition also linted by S16, 🤔 ELI5 linter.)
> - Joel Chan (SP-24 W04): "idea of intelligence augmentation is deeply rooted in work on cybernetics". Pointers: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_amplification and "Return of cybernetics" (*Nature Machine Intelligence*, https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-019-0100-x). There is likely "more control theory stuff here" worth considering.
> - The cooperation-vs.-automation framing in [[@kochMayAIDesign2019]] ("how to support *cooperation* (augmentation) rather than automation in computational support for ideation") is the same stance applied to ideation (S06, 🏺 Archaeologist, SP-24 W02).
