## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Geiger lays out forms of opacity in machine-learning systems and adds a fourth (summarized by S16, Distiller, SP-24 W08):
> - **Intentional secrecy**: algorithms kept confidential for competitive advantage or IP reasons.
> - **Technical illiteracy**: the knowledge gap between end users and the people who build the algorithms. Even openly documented algorithms can be opaque to people without the expertise.
> - **Inherent opacity**: complex models (e.g., deep neural nets) are black boxes, so we can't interpret why they make specific decisions.
> - **Geiger's extension**: the difficulty of understanding how systems are embedded in, and influence, institutional or organizational culture. At Wikipedia, this is the challenge of navigating the policies, bots, and norms that govern participation.
> ![[uZAIXoS6xr.png]]
> Key source: [[@geigerOpeningBlackBox2017]]. The first three forms are the ones Geiger takes from earlier work on ML opacity; the notes do not record the citation.
