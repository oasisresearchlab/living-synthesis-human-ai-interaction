## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Two metacognitive capacities [[@guptaFosteringCollectiveIntelligence]] say a human-AI collective needs (S11, Distiller, SP-24 W11):
> - **Machine theory of mind (MToM)**: AI agents that can predict other agents' cognitive states, starting with desires, intentions, and beliefs.
> - **Human theory of machine mind (HToMM)**: how a person understands a machine's cognition. S11: "I think this is where a lot of anthropomorphization happens."
> Both are presented as important for the emergence of collective intelligence.
## Key Examples
> [!info] What are some key examples of the pattern instantiated in artifacts/systems? Later we can link particularly instructive/illustrative ones via the "Instantiates" discourse relation.

Claude:
> - CogToM (Nguyen and Gonzalez 2022): an observer model that simulates a human-like theory of mind, bounded by limits on human memory, built on [[THE - Instance-Based Learning Theory (IBLT)]]. It can make inferences that agree with human observers' judgments on the same task (S11, Distiller, SP-24 W11).
## Other Notes
Claude:
> - S11 noted that standard theory-of-mind models do not capture human biases that come from the limits of the human mind; CogToM is an attempt to address that.
