## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Bennett et al. propose four aspects that help clarify what a given work means by agency and autonomy: "1. issues of self-causality and personal identity; 2. the experience and material expression of autonomy and agency; 3. particular timescales; and 4. emphasis on independence and interdependence" (p. 12, per [[@bennett2023howa]]; session reading [[@bennettHowDoesHCI2023]]).
> S05's distillation (Distiller, SP-24 W12):
> - **Self-causality**: the level and directness of the user's causal involvement, in execution (material involvement in tasks and outcomes) and decision (choice). Operationalized via button presses, narrative choice, control in video games. Diminished agency: no involvement, flexibility, or adaptation in execution.
> - **Identity**: self-congruence (outcomes align with one's values and goals, regardless of self-causality) and self-construction (effects on one's identity, values, and goals).
> - **Material vs. experiential**: the material expression of agency and the factors that impinge on it (coercion, lacking economic means, power), vs. the felt sense of agency (sense of ownership, haptic feedback, timing of events).
> - **Time scale**: micro-interactions vs. longer scales, up to a life; and the relationships between scales.
> - **Independence and interdependence**: the self's relationship to its environment.
