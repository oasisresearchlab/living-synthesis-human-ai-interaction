## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Find an effective practice that pockets of skilled practitioners already use informally, then build a tool that makes that practice systematic and available to others. The novelty is not the practice but its systematization and scaffolding.
> - In the SP-25 W06 discussion of [[ART - DreamSheets]], a participant argued the tool was not novel: "It's so simple."
