## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Introduced in [[@ehsanSeamfulXAIOperationalizing2022]]. Mistakes in AI systems are inevitable, arising from technical limitations and sociotechnical gaps. Black-boxing AI can make the user experience seamless, but hiding the **seams** risks disempowering users from mitigating fallout from AI mistakes. Seamful XAI strategically reveals sociotechnical and infrastructural mismatches, rather than only tackling algorithmic opaqueness, to augment explainability and user agency.
>
> The paper (1) transfers the concept of "seams" to the AI context and (2) develops a design process that **helps stakeholders design with seams**, explored with 43 AI practitioners and users in a scenario-based co-design activity. (From the paper's abstract, shared in the SP-24 seminar)
