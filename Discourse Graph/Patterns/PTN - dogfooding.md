## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> Building something and having its makers use it first ("eating your own dog food"), then circulating it to willing testers and assuming it can scale. Joel Chan's notes on Mary L. Gray's CSCW 2021 keynote, "Dump the Dogfood: Towards a New Theory of Power in Computing" (no recording), cast it as computing's unquestioned assumption that "prototyping is the first step". (Joel Chan, Academic Researcher, SP-24 W13)
> - Origin stories: Alpo ads with celebrities, and a study of dogfooding at Motorola (https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1109/ICGSE.2019.000-6). (SP-24 W13)
> - It leads to the "minimum viable product", which raises the question: "minimal for whom? viable for whom?" (SP-24 W13)
