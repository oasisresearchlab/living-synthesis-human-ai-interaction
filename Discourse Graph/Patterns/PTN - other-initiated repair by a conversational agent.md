## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> In conversation-analysis terms, "other-initiated repair" means the listener, not the speaker, starts the repair of a misunderstanding. S05 suggested reading positive friction this way: "the conversational agent will initiate repair rather than relying on the users themselves to do self-repair when miscommunication occurs." (S05, Connector thread, SP-25 W14)
> - In the transcript, a student described how repair can sound: initiating it ("I didn't understand, can you clarify?") or doing the repair for the other person ("do you mean this, do you mean that?"). (SP-25 seminar discussion, SP-25 W14, [00:36:08])
> - Grounded in Clark and Wilkes-Gibbs (1986), "Referring as a collaborative process", which describes the moves that follow an initial noun phrase to initiate or complete a repair. (S05, SP-25 W14)
