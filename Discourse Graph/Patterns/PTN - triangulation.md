## Description/Intuition and Key Sources
> [!info] What is the key intuition for the pattern? Include links to key/canonical sources.

Claude:
> "the combination of two or more data sources, investigators, methodological approaches, theoretical perspectives (Denzin, 1970; Kimchi, Polivka, & Stevenson, 1991), or analytical methods (Kimchi et al., 1991) within the same study", quoted in Braggaar et al. (2023). (S13, Distiller, SP-24 W15)
> ![[Mii64uFzdD.png]]
> S13's glosses: data sources as different samples; investigators as independent replication; methods as mixed methods; analyses as different statistical tests. Joel Chan's example for theories: misinformation scholars whose theories have overlapping constructs.

## Key Examples
> [!info] What are some key examples of the pattern instantiated in artifacts/systems? Later we can link particularly instructive/illustrative ones via the "Instantiates" discourse relation.

Claude:
> For dialogue systems, Braggaar et al. suggest combining (S13, SP-24 W15):
> - asking people about their experience during interaction (concurrent think-aloud) and after (surveys, questionnaires);
> - spoken or written interaction data and metadata (number of turns, response length, time on task);
> - unspoken data (video of nonverbal responses, biometrics).
