"Neither automated suggestions nor direct manipulation play a strictly dominant role. Rather, we have observed that a fluent interleaving of both modalities can enable more productive, yet flexible, work." (SP-24 notes, February 12th, 2024)

> Results reported in the SP-24 notes:
> - Quality: PTM > post-editing > manual translation.
> - Efficiency: post-editing >~ PTM > manual translation.
> - More consistent but less creative translations. Translators: "the translator is less susceptible to be creative"; "[MT] distracts from my own original translation process by putting words in [my] head".
